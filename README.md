# 📊 Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence project built using **Power BI** to analyze sales performance across different product categories, customer regions, and time periods.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful insights, enabling business users to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify sales trends, evaluate customer satisfaction, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Monitor revenue and profit trends over time.
- Compare sales across different product categories.
- Evaluate customer satisfaction using ratings.
- Analyze regional sales performance.
- Track order quantity and customer reviews.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

### Revenue Performance

- Generated **$32.87M** in total revenue.
- Maintained stable sales throughout the year.

### Customer Satisfaction

- Nearly **70%** of customers are either **Satisfied** or **Highly Satisfied**.
- Around **18%** of customers are Unsatisfied, indicating opportunities for service improvement.

### Regional Performance

- Revenue is distributed consistently across:
  - Asia
  - Europe
  - Middle East
  - North America

### Sales Trend

- Sales remained relatively stable throughout the year.
- Highest revenue observed around **August**.
- Lower sales observed during **February** and **November**.

### Product Performance

- Beauty and Books generated among the highest revenue.
- Product categories contributed fairly evenly, indicating a diversified sales portfolio.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase promotional campaigns during low-performing months.
- Investigate reasons behind lower customer satisfaction.
- Focus marketing efforts on top-performing product categories.
- Optimize discount strategies to maximize revenue while maintaining profitability.
- Continue expanding in regions with consistent revenue growth.
